How online consultations work

  1. 1

    Message me on WhatsApp

    I’ll answer your questions, share pricing and payment options, and we’ll find the best time.

  2. 2

    Consultation by video call

    At our scheduled time I’ll send you the Google Meet link. Just click, no app or install needed. The first consultation lasts up to 2 hours.

  3. 3

    Personalized meal plan

    Within 3 business days you get your plan, built around your routine and the food you enjoy.

  4. 4

    45-minute follow-ups

    We review, adjust what didn’t flow and track your progress. You change, and the plan changes with you.

What changes compared to in person?

Almost nothing. Same consultation, same plan, same dedication, same price. No “lite” version for online clients.

The difference is the body assessment. At my office in Caxias do Sul, Brazil, I use bioimpedance and skinfold measurements. Online, body composition is estimated with AI, using a scientifically validated method, without leaving home.

If you have recent blood work, just send it before the consultation and I’ll go through it with you.

Frequently asked questions

Does an online nutrition consultation really work?

Yes. The online consultation is the same as in person: the first one lasts up to 2 hours so I can understand your routine, history and goals, and you get a fully personalized meal plan. I'm a registered nutritionist in Brazil (CRN2 12907P).

Do I need to install an app?

No. The consultation is a video call on Google Meet: I send the link at our scheduled time and you just click, on your phone or computer.

How is the body assessment done remotely?

I use an AI-based estimate of body composition, with a scientifically validated method, without you leaving home.

How much does an online consultation cost?

The price is the same as in person. What changes is whether you prefer a single consultation or a follow-up package. Message me on WhatsApp and I'll share pricing and payment options, no strings attached.

How soon do I get my meal plan?

Within 3 business days after the consultation.
